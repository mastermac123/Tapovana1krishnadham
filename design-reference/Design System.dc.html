Design system

The language before the building.

Every rule that follows exists so the society’s digital home can be quiet, exact and unhurried — a document you read rather than an interface you operate.

01 — Palette

Five values, no more. Green carries authority, champagne carries occasion, ivory carries everything else.

No gradient may exceed a single‑value shade. No blue. No saturated hue anywhere. Green on ivory 11.4:1. Charcoal on ivory 13.2:1. Gold is never used for body text.

Display type sits at 300 weight. Inter never exceeds 500. Uppercase only for labels, and only with 0.22–0.34em tracking. Numerals are lining, tabular in every table and document list.
03 — Grid & space

A twelve column field at 1440, 104px margins, 32px gutters. Whitespace is the primary material.

180Section rhythmVertical air between two major sections.
96Block rhythmHeading to content, row to row.
32GutterColumn gap, card gap, form gap.
8Base unitEvery value is a multiple. No exceptions.
Composition rules
Text columns occupy 5 or 6 of 12. Never the full width. Images are full‑bleed or exactly half the field — nothing in between. One idea per screen. If two compete, they become two sections.
04 — Motion

Six curves, used everywhere, and nothing else. Motion explains structure; it never decorates.

Content revealexpo.out1250 msMasked rise, 70 ms per word, one block at a time.
Curtain & page changeexpo.inOut1100 msGreen panel wipes across, new page revealed beneath. No white flash.
Hoverpower3.out550 msInterruptible, always reversible mid‑flight. Never a bounce.
Image scrublinearscroll‑linked1.14 over‑scale settling to 1.02, counter‑drift parallax.
Rule drawpower2.inOut900 msDividers grow from the left as their section arrives.
Cursor & smooth scrolllerp 0.12continuousRing trails the dot; scroll eased to 1.05 s of inertia.
Only transform, clip‑path and opacity animate. Everything is composited; 60 fps is a floor, not a target. Nothing pops. Nothing overshoots. If an animation cannot be explained in one sentence, it is removed. Reduced‑motion preference disables every reveal and lands the page fully composed.
